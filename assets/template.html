Lemma 2 · the diamond

The printed induction is not well-founded. In the Me-Pro/Me-Var case the induction hypothesis is applied to α₀ ⟶≡ α₂, built from the context reduction by Ct-Ann and weakening — a hypothesis of the lemma, not a subderivation of either argument, and of unrelated size.

Four families of measures fail. The obstruction is stable: Me-Pro needs a variable lookup to outweigh its annotation at the current stack, while Me-FOp needs a stack entry to outweigh the variable it becomes.

Cites Lemma 2, so the two are sequential rather than mutual. Everything else its proof uses — Proposition 18 repaired, Lemmas 19, 30, 32 and 36 — is proved here, leaving six diagram cases over a black-box Lemma 2.
